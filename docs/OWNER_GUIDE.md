# Swarna Live: owner and staff guide

## Signing in

1. Open **www.swarnalive.com**.
2. Type the **business email and password**. Only the owner knows these.
3. Tap your **name** and type your **6-digit PIN**.

Each person has their own PIN. If nobody uses the app for a while, your profile locks itself. Just enter your PIN again.

- **Switch profile**: lets the next person use their own PIN. The shop stays signed in.
- **Log out of business account**: signs the whole shop out on this device (at closing time, or on a shared device).

## First day (owner)

1. **Save your recovery codes.** Print them or write them down. Keep them at home, not in the shop. If you forget the password, one code lets you back in.
2. **Settings → Rates.** Enter today's **24K gold** price per gram and **999 silver** price per gram. Do this every time the price changes. Swarna Live never fetches prices from the internet.
3. **Settings → Business.** Shop name, phone, address and logo (shown on receipts).
4. **Settings → Buyback rules.** Default percentage (60%) and the lowest and highest percentage staff may use.
5. **Employees → Add staff.** Name, role, what they may do, and a temporary PIN. They choose their own PIN the first time.

## Making a buyback estimate

1. Tap **New Buyback**.
2. Customer name and mobile number.
3. **Gold** or **Silver**, then the purity (24K, 22K, 20K, 18K, 14K; silver 999).
4. Item type and a short description.
5. Put the item on the scale. Type the **gross weight**, then any **stone/other weight** to take off.
6. Check the buyback %.
7. Tap **Take photo**. The photo must show **the jewellery and the scale reading together**. Tap **Retake** if it is blurry.
8. Check the estimate on the right and tap **Save estimate**.

You get an order number like **26-0001**. Tap **Print receipt** to give the customer a printed estimate.

If the rate was changed while you were filling in the form, Swarna Live shows the new offer and asks you to check it before saving.

**Important:** these are *estimates*. "Accepted" means the customer agreed to the estimate; it does **not** mean money was paid. The photo is a record; it does not prove weight or purity. The final value is subject to physical inspection and purity confirmation.

## Fixing mistakes

- Wrong name, phone, item type, description or notes: open the transaction → **Correct details** and give a reason.
- Wrong weight, purity or percentage: **Change status → Cancelled** (with a reason) and make a new estimate. Saved figures never change, so the history stays honest.
- Every change is recorded with who did it, when, why, and the old and new values.

## Google backup (optional, owner only)

Settings → **Backups & Integrations**:

1. **Connect Google Account** and allow Drive and Sheets.
2. **Create a backup folder for me**.
3. Paste a Google Sheets link → **Check links** → choose "Create a new worksheet" → tick the box → **Save Destinations**.
4. **Test Connection**.

After that, every saved estimate's photo goes to your Drive folder and a row goes to your sheet. To get an Excel file: open the sheet, then **File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)**.

If Google stops working, Swarna Live **keeps saving** everything. The dashboard shows "Reconnect needed". Reconnect, then tap **Back up missed records**.

## Staff problems

- **Forgot PIN**: the owner opens Employees → **Reset PIN**.
- **Staff member leaves**: Employees → **Disable**. They are signed out immediately.
- **Lost or stolen phone**: Employees → **Sign out everywhere** for that person. Also change the business password (Settings → Security), which signs out every device.
